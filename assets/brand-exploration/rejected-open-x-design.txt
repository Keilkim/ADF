Design a new ORIGINAL capital X logomark for XDF, a contemporary desktop document workspace. No words or labels, no shading, no texture, no gradients, no shadows, no enclosing tile, no presentations or mockups. No ordinary crossed lines, no close-button icon, no petals, no arrows, no sportswear swoosh. Make an original finished logomark with a memorable silhouette.
