XDF logo research, round 2 — 2026-10-01

Deliverable: us-brand-references-v2.png.
This is a visual comparison of actual brand references, not generated XDF designs.
The accompanying observations are design interpretations for XDF.

StockX
https://stockx.com/about/brand-assets/
X mark extracted from the SVG labeled StockX Logo served by stockx.com.
Focus: asymmetric crossing, controlled width and recognizable silhouette.

SpaceX
https://www.spacex.com/
Header wordmark SVG extracted from the official homepage.
Focus: unequal diagonals, a single directional curve and custom lettering.

A24
https://a24films.com/
Header wordmark SVG extracted from the official homepage.
Focus: distinctive letter architecture and spacing across a short name.

Dropbox
https://brand.dropbox.com/logo
Official glyph image loaded from the brand guidelines page:
https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66c503d081b2f012369fc5d2/674000d6c0a42d41f8c331be_dropbox-2-logo-png-transparent-p-500.png
Focus: repeated planar geometry and meaningful central negative space.

Oakley
https://stores.oakley.com/us/ca/los-angeles/100-citadel-dr
Official store-listing logo image:
https://a.mktgcdn.com/p/dvXHxfOT0OillcoMXZj9WQJrCrxOx-XSvZa0jTkhNRM/1730x1730.jpg

Xsolla
https://www.pentagram.com/work/xsolla
Brand application image from the identity designers' case study.
Focus: consistent geometric language across mark, wordmark and product UI.
