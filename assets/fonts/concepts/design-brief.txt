The user rejected square, modular, stencil-like, skeletal and futuristic lettering. Keep these modifications around 3–5% of a stroke thickness, subtle and consistent, never a repeating decorative gimmick. Typographic structure and reading clarity dominate.
Composition: landscape approximately 1536x1024, flat front-facing printed specimen graphic on pure warm white, extremely crisp black text, generous disciplined margins, restrained hairline dividers. Small supplied vermilion XDF logo at upper left; family label and category at upper right. A large Korean sentence, one medium English sentence, a compact alphabet/sample row, and lower down an official-document fragment alongside 3 magnified glyphs that reveal the small custom details. No perspective, no page photography, no shadows, no grey UI cards, no ornamental objects. Do not clutter the board with tiny text.

This restrained geometry is inspired by XDF, without copying its inflated logo proportions.
